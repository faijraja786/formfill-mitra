PAN application preparation · Step 1

PAN application

Choose the PAN card delivery preference before preparing the application. This preference will be saved with your preparation record.

Important: This is your preferred output format. The authorized PAN provider may ask you to select it again on its official application page. FormFill Mitra does not collect the PAN government/provider fee.
Before proceeding: This page is for a new PAN application only. If the applicant already has a PAN, do not apply for another one. Use the official correction/reprint service instead. Applicants under 18 should complete the application with a parent or legal guardian.

What happens next?

1. Upload available documents and select saved documents (Personal users). 2. Review applicant details and correct any mistakes. 3. Review the complete preparation record. 4. Open the authorized PAN provider, complete any remaining official fields, verify OTP/CAPTCHA if requested, pay the fee directly there, and submit.

Our website cannot securely pre-fill or submit the provider's external application. Please verify all details and current fees on the official provider page.
